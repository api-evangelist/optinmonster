eCommerce lead generation is the process of capturing contact information from online shoppers before they leave your store, so you can bring them back and convert them into buyers through email, SMS, or retargeting. Most store owners treat this as an afterthought. They drive traffic, watch 97% of it disappear, and blame the ads.
